# Icarus-S

The public side of Icarus. One argument, told in scenes: how a rocket comes down.

A scene is a picture you can move, a chart drawn from the same samples, and a conversation beside them. The page holds the numbers. A reply can move the time, or ask for a series that is already in the buffer. It does not invent rows.

## A state and its rate

The first scene is a bead on a rod.

Height is the state. The rate is how fast that height is changing. At the top, and again at the bottom, the bead turns around and the rate is zero. Play runs that clock in real time. The chart draws only as far as the bead has gone, and the dot at the end of the line is the sample you are on.

The parts that follow are listed beside the picture: the loop, the turn, the vehicle, and the descent. They are named so the argument has a shape. They are not drawn yet.
