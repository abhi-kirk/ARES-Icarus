## Kit contents

Four cardboard tubes: 23" slotted booster, 11" payload bay, 38 mm motor tube, and a coupler.
Polypropylene nose cone with a molded eyelet. Three 1/8" plywood fins, burned PK-48, with
through-the-wall tabs. Three centering rings burned FWD, MID and AFT. Bulkhead assembly burned
BA-3.90. 36" parachute, 15 ft nylon shock cord with sewn loops. Logo decals.

Hardware: **two SCM-3 eyebolts** (1/4"-20, one bagged with the FWD centering ring, one with the
bulkhead), each with two washers and a nut. 
**Two rail buttons** with two wood screws. 
**One quick link**, 660 lb. **MR-1 retention: two Z-clips, two T-nuts and screws.**

The instruction sheet's parts list names one eyebolt and no quick link. The bag holds more than
the list says; build from the bag.

### Condition and fit

- Every tube end rolls true. No crushing, no out-of-round.
- Fins flat, no warp or delamination.
- **Coupler into the payload tube: snug, light friction.** No shimming needed and drag
  separation is not a concern on this airframe. Re-check after paint, since primer on the
  coupler OD can tighten it past usable.
- **The FWD centering ring and the bulkhead plate are both factory-drilled** for their
  eyebolts. No drilling needed for the recovery anchors.
- **The AFT centering ring is factory-drilled with the two T-nut holes, 180° apart.** Confirm
  during dry fit that neither lands on a fin slot; the instructions warn about this and factory
  drilling does not guarantee it.

All nine order lines received and matching the packing slip: LP-42 42" parachute, 6/0 swivel,
three CEC-3 quick links, an SCM-3 eyebolt, KV-125-8 Kevlar, FRB-M 12"×12" blanket, MMA-2

**Kevlar has sewn loops on both ends.** Use it uncut and unknotted, since knots cost roughly half of
Kevlar's strength and none are needed.

assembles as designed.

coupler adapter, assembled by epoxying the rings onto the tube and the tube into the adapter.
It has no role until there is a 29 mm flight. What the MR-1 Z-clips retain through the adapter
is unresolved and can be settled then.

### Paint

**Scheme, decided 2026-10-11.** Booster and payload bay are fluorescent orange. Fins and nose cone are gloss black.

**Black, bought 2026-10-11:** Rust-Oleum Stops Rust Spray Paint, Gloss Black, 12 oz, number 7779830. One can covers the fins and the nose cone. The nose cone gets plastic bonding primer first, then this black. The fins get filler primer, then this black.

**Filler primer** (already on hand) goes on the booster, the payload bay, and the fins. It fills the tube spirals and the wood grain. Sand it back. It does not go on the nose cone, and it does not go on the outside of the coupler, or the coupler will not slide into the booster.

**Orange, decided:** the fluorescent already on hand is the top coat. Rust-Oleum Specialty Fluorescent Bright Neon, red-orange, 11 oz (`15`, part 342568). It fades over seasons. That was accepted.

It will not go on the grey filler primer. Buy a can of **gloss white** spray and put that on the booster and payload bay after the filler primer is sanded. Then the fluorescent goes on the white. One can of white is enough. The gloss orange enamel (214084) is not being bought.

The 2X Ultra Cover line is not a candidate for the orange.

### Breathing protection

The mesh dust mask on hand (`24`) covers **sanding**, which is most of the mask hours in this
build.

It does not cover **spraying**. Its activated carbon is an odour-reduction pad, not an
organic-vapour bed, so it weakens the smell of solvent without removing much of it and deletes
the only warning cue. An organic-vapor respirator is now on hand for spraying. Keep using the mesh mask for sanding.

A short beard breaks the face seal on any tight-fitting respirator, so all spraying happens
outdoors regardless. A partial seal over a real OV cartridge still beats a perfect seal over a
carbon pad. Store it in a sealed ziplock; OV media absorbs solvent from ambient air on the
shelf.

### Markings

The rocket is named **Icarus I**. The Roman numeral marks it as the first of the series. It carries no LOC branding, so the kit decals stay in the box. The name goes on as cut vinyl over the colour coat, then clear coat on top. Order from [lettering.com](https://lettering.com/products/custom-vinyl-lettering/), outdoor vinyl, black, about 1.5 to 2 inches tall. Black works on either orange. Production is about a day, shipping about a week, so order in October. Letter colour does not need to wait on the final paint can. **Do not etch or engrave the airframe**, which removes material from a structural cardboard tube for decoration.

Put a name and phone number on the airframe for recovery.
